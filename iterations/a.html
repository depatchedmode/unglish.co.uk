One set, four addresses

Unglish a gives each word its own melody. Arbiglish, baglish, optiglish and zorglish use the same generator on different chains. The names are local; the rules are shared.

Released on 6 January 2024. Above is the artwork exactly as minted, the very files stored on Arweave, run with each token’s own inputs from the chain. Nothing has been tidied.

About the archive

Every token’s metadata records the exact address its artwork was rendered from. This page keeps a copy of that artwork and those addresses, checked on 21 October 2026, so it needs neither Highlight nor a blockchain to show them. Voices belong to your browser, so a word may not sound as it did at the mint.
